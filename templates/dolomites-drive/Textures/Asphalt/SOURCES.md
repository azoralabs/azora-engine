# Alpine asphalt

`base-color.png` was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. The runtime
`surface.aztex` contains the base color, a normal map derived from aggregate
detail, and packed roughness, ambient occlusion, height and metalness.

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless base-color texture for PBR game asphalt, square orthographic tile covering 2 x 2 metres. Generate a high-detail photographic sample of compacted charcoal-grey asphalt from a real alpine highway, aged but maintained, with tightly packed crushed basalt and limestone aggregate and subtle fine bitumen mottling. Perfect straight-down view, uniform flat diffuse overcast illumination, balanced neutral dark grey, even detail right up to the edges. Tile seamlessly on both axes. No road markings, seams, potholes, cracks, verges, shadows, perspective or text. The texture must fill the complete canvas.
