# Alpine PBR surfaces

The three base colors were generated as overhead, evenly lit, seamless surface images. For each set, normal, roughness, ambient occlusion and 16-bit height were derived from the texture signal. Opaque dielectric surfaces use zero metalness, 4% specular reflectance and full opacity. `surface.aztexture` packs base color, normal and an RGBA surface map (roughness, AO, height, metalness).
